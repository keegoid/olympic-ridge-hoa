We hope everyone in Olympic Ridge has a fun and safe 4th of July.

Please remember how dry conditions have been and use extra care with fireworks. Follow [Mason County's posted fireworks hours and restrictions](https://www.masoncountywa.gov/departments/community_development/fire_marshal/fireworks.php), keep safety in mind throughout the day, and clean up after the celebration is over.
