This is a public summary of the January 16 community meeting, not approved minutes. The source reports a vote approving the 2026 budget; other projects described below were discussed or assigned for follow-up.

## Topics discussed

- **2026 budget and assessments:** The 2026 budget was reported approved by vote. The annual assessment increased from $940 to $1,000, a $60 difference, to address rising utility and maintenance costs. Initial invoices used the prior amount, so supplemental billing was discussed.
- **Association operations:** The Board recapped elections, safety improvements, website work, landscaping, reserve needs, and an upcoming election for one open seat.
- **Storm pond and vegetation:** The group discussed pond cleanup, insurance and liability concerns, possible signage, Scotch broom removal, and tree management near the pond. Scotch broom work was discussed as a possible added landscaping cost.
- **Roads and fire lanes:** The Board described enforcement of fire-lane parking restrictions and discussed entry-road striping, possible speed bumps, snow removal, and shared maintenance responsibility with a neighboring property owner. Exact boundaries remained to be checked against plat maps.
- **Community projects:** The group discussed mailbox-area landscaping, a long-term playground idea, a community sign, safety bollards already installed on a service road, and phased mailbox maintenance.
- **Property guidance:** The Board discussed keeping waste containers off sidewalks, using ACC requests to document property changes, and the absence of a garage-sale restriction in the governing documents as described at the meeting.

## Follow-up items mentioned

- **Management company:** Send supplemental $60 invoices where needed, provide requested account statements, send plat maps to the Board, and schedule the annual audit.
- **Board:** Review the road and pond-area property boundaries, tree-management options, pond signage, and possible road striping or speed bumps.
- **Board:** Consider Scotch broom and pond-area maintenance, and review the next phase of mailbox-area landscaping against available funds.
- **Board and management company:** Continue fire-lane enforcement and ACC processing under the existing procedures.
- **Board:** Explore volunteer help with painting the community sign.

The source does not establish completion of these follow-up items.
