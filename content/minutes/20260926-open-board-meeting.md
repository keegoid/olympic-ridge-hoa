This summary describes the open portion of the meeting. It is not approved minutes. The meeting notes do not record formal motions or votes.

Three directors participated, and a quorum was noted during the meeting. Community members also attended.

## Topics discussed

- **Community communications:** The board discussed maintaining a private contact list for meeting notices and other HOA communications. A website form for residents to give or withdraw consent to email communications was described during the meeting. Resident contact information will remain private.
- **Shared road maintenance:** A board representative reported plans to meet with the neighboring apartment property's new owners to review shared road maintenance responsibilities. No cost-sharing agreement was reported.
- **Water billing and landscaping:** A board representative planned to discuss water billing with the water commissioner and reported a negotiated reduction to a landscaping vendor's final bill. The meeting did not establish that payment or closeout was complete.
- **Property inspections and appeals:** Directors discussed the need for consistent inspections and enforcement. Residents were reminded that they may submit a violation notice appeal through the management company for board review.
- **Parking and pedestrian access:** Directors discussed consistent parking enforcement and the importance of keeping sidewalks and crosswalks clear. The discussion of individual vehicles and residents is omitted here.
- **Curb and crosswalk markings:** Directors discussed the cost of proposed painting. They did not commit to the project for the remainder of 2026; additional pricing and possible consideration in the 2027 budget remain open.
- **Common-area land:** The board discussed property taxes and maintenance costs for association-owned land as inputs to the 2027 budget. Amounts were still being reviewed.
- **Lawn care:** Directors discussed general lawn maintenance during water restrictions and shared optional lawn-care tips. Residents with questions about a specific notice may use the appeal process.
- **Community gathering and notices:** A fall neighborhood gathering was proposed, with the date and details still to be determined. A director also planned to research the cost of additional bulletin boards near the community mailboxes.
