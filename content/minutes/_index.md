An archive of public HOA records, including open board meeting minutes, owner meeting minutes, and community walk-through notes.

Prior monthly board meeting summaries have been removed while the Board transitions to noticed, open monthly board meetings. New board meeting records will be posted after open meetings going forward.

The Board's regular monthly open board meeting is scheduled for the last Saturday of each month at 9:00 AM Pacific Time. Meeting agendas, location or remote access details, and any executive-session topics should be posted in the calendar entry and provided through the meeting schedule or a separate notice before the meeting.

Closed executive-session details are not general meeting minutes and may be omitted, limited, or redacted where Washington law protects privacy, attorney-client privilege, vendor negotiations, enforcement matters, or individual owner files. Owner meetings remain labeled separately as community or annual meeting minutes.
