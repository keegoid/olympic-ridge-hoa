An archive of public summaries of open board meetings, owner meetings, and community walk-throughs. These posts are not approved meeting minutes. Owners may request association records from the Board.

The Board's regular monthly open board meeting is scheduled for the last Saturday of each month at 9:00 AM Pacific Time. Meeting agendas, location or remote access details, and any executive-session topics should be posted in the calendar entry and provided through the meeting schedule or a separate notice before the meeting.

These summaries omit private or confidential details, including executive-session discussion. Owner meetings are labeled separately as community or annual meeting summaries.
