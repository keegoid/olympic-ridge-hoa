This summary describes the open portion of the October 31 board meeting. It is not approved minutes. Four directors and community members participated. Individual owner, account, property, and other confidential matters are omitted.

## Topics discussed

- **Budget transparency:** The board approved a public budget and reserves page by a recorded vote of four. The approved scope includes the ratified budget, aggregate spending compared with budget, dated operating and reserve balances, current dues, a reserve-study summary, and approved project updates. Before publication, a board-designated reviewer must check accuracy, dates, and privacy. Proposals and estimates must be clearly labeled; updates are planned quarterly and after material changes.
- **2027 budget planning:** Management was preparing the draft budget for board review. Directors discussed common-area maintenance, property taxes, reserve needs, and efforts to control expenses. No 2027 budget, assessment amount, or dues increase was adopted at this meeting.
- **Common-area tax and water updates:** Directors reported that the taxable value for certain common-area tracts had been corrected to zero through 2026, with a related tax refund discussed. They cautioned that the 2027 outcome may differ. A small water-district refund was also reported.
- **Landscaping and winter work:** The board discussed continuing the current service arrangement, including winter maintenance and planned pond-area cleanup. A detailed comparison with seasonal pricing had not been completed; potential savings were discussed as estimates rather than established results.
- **Mulch proposal:** A quote of approximately $5,280 was presented for common-area mulch installation, with spring suggested as a suitable time. Consideration was deferred pending the 2027 budget. No installation or expenditure was approved.
- **Speed and pedestrian safety:** Directors reviewed preliminary options for an entrance speed-limit and radar sign. Further work will compare wired-power pricing, installation, and any permit requirements. No sign purchase or change in the community speed limit was approved.
- **Shared road maintenance:** Directors reported a meeting with neighboring property representatives about road maintenance and possible winter-service cooperation. Cost estimates and proposed allocations remained outstanding; no new cost-sharing agreement was approved.
- **Covered bus-stop proposal:** A covered waiting area was discussed as a possible future project. Location, cost, maintenance, and priority still require review. No construction or expenditure was approved.
- **Clear sidewalks and community information:** Residents were reminded to keep sidewalks and fire lanes clear, including when placing collection bins or hosting visitors. Directors discussed a possible bin-warning form, continued verification of electronic-notice requests with management, and mailbox bulletin boards to improve access to community information.
- **Board project tracking:** Directors supported maintaining a shared list of active and proposed projects so members can volunteer, identify who is working on each item, and follow up consistently.

## Follow-up items mentioned

- Obtain wired-power radar-sign quotes and research installation and permit costs.
- Prepare the approved public budget information for accuracy and privacy review before publication.
- Review the 2027 draft budget and revisit the mulch proposal when the budget is available.
- Follow up on shared-road cost estimates and compile prior road-maintenance and improvement costs.
- Develop the covered bus-stop proposal for later consideration.
- Explore a collection-bin warning form and tracking approach.
- Continue validating electronic-notice requests with management and evaluate mailbox bulletin boards.
- Create a shared board project list with responsibilities and progress.

Only the budget-transparency approval described above is reported here as a formal vote on these public project topics. Proposals, estimates, and follow-up items should not be read as completed work or spending authorizations.
