This is a public summary of the July 25 open board meeting, not approved minutes. Four of five directors and community members participated by Google Meet; a board quorum was present. The call disconnected during the meeting, and participants rejoined to finish the business. Personal and property-specific matters are omitted.

**Later event correction:** The Neighborhood Block Party discussed below was moved from the originally recorded September 13 date to September 12, 2026, and was then postponed because of weather. As of the September 11 [event notice](/announcements/neighborhood-block-party/), no replacement date had been announced. The meeting discussion reflects the plans at the time.

## Topics discussed

- **2026 budget and costs:** The Board reviewed approximately $144,000 in budgeted annual income and $113,000 in operating expenses. At midyear, about $135,000 had been received and about $58,000 spent. Mailing, insurance, common-area repairs, and unbudgeted property taxes were among the cost pressures discussed.
- **Collections and reserves:** The Board discussed overdue assessments without identifying owners. The reserve study showed a fully funded annual contribution about $22,000 above the budgeted amount. No dues increase was proposed or voted on at this meeting; clearer financial charts were requested for future discussion.
- **Certificate of deposit — approved:** After owner comments, all four participating directors voted in favor of moving $40,000 from operating checking into a certificate of deposit. The motion was restated and confirmed after the meeting connection was restored. A $30,000 alternative did not receive a separate vote. The term was left to be determined.
- **Communication tool trial — approved:** Directors voted by show of hands for a one-month, no-cost trial of a shared communication tool. Other suggested tools were to be evaluated during the trial.
- **Landscaping and road responsibilities:** The Board discussed damage-recovery efforts involving a former landscaping vendor, the return to the prior vendor, and which private roads, parcels, and common areas the Association maintains.
- **Parking and road safety:** Owners raised concerns about temporary no-parking signs, fire-lane access, a bus stop, blind corners, and possible curb markings. The Board agreed to review sign placement and research alternatives; no parking-sign vote was recorded.
- **ACC and communication:** The Board discussed private follow-up on an architectural matter, more visible meeting outreach, electronic-notice consent, and the planned neighborhood gathering.

## Follow-up items mentioned

- **Board and management company:** Carry out the approved certificate transfer and prepare financial charts on yields, reserves, and future allocations.
- **Board:** Check parcel ownership and the unbudgeted property-tax treatment with the management company; continue documented collections and vendor damage-recovery work.
- **Board:** Review temporary sign placement against governing documents and price road striping and curb markings, including safety-sensitive areas.
- **Board:** Set up the approved communication-tool trial and evaluate alternatives; follow up privately on the architectural matter.
- **Board and volunteers:** Continue outreach about meetings and electronic-notice consent. The planned block party was later postponed as noted above.

These are actions recorded or proposed at the July meeting; this page does not verify their present-day completion.
